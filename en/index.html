Questions

What buyers ask us

What does Evidentelilás do?

Evidentelilás is a garment factory in Leiria, Portugal, that manufactures clothing to order for brands: coats, overcoats, trench coats, blazers and luxury uniforms, under the client's label. The house has been in the trade since 1989.

Do you work with brands outside Portugal?

Yes. Most of Evidentelilás' production leaves Portugal, for high and very high-end brands. We work in English and Portuguese.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The minimum order depends on the garment and the fabric. Tell us the garment, the quantity and the timing, and Evidentelilás replies with feasibility and next steps.

Do you sell to the public?

No. Evidentelilás only produces to order, for brands. Our own collection, Lilás, is sold to stockists.

Can I visit the factory?

Yes, by appointment. The factory is at Rua de Santo António 790, 2420-316 Leiria, Portugal, open Monday to Friday, 8:30 to 13:00 and 14:00 to 17:30.
