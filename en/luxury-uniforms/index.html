Questions

What buyers ask us

What are luxury uniforms?

Luxury uniforms are uniforms made like tailoring: chosen fabric, a tailor's cut and the same finish in every size. Evidentelilás manufactures them to order, in series, at its factory in Leiria, Portugal.

Which uniform garments do you make?

Evidentelilás makes uniforms and suits: blazers, jackets, overcoats and trousers, in your brand's fabric, colour and label.

Which companies do you make uniforms for?

Evidentelilás makes uniforms for companies whose staff are the face of the brand, in Portugal and abroad. Luxury retail, aviation and entertainment have all come through the house; client names stay confidential.

Do you also make healthcare uniforms?

Yes. In 2020, during the pandemic, the factory adapted its line in one week and made 9,000 medical items for the City of Leiria.

What is the minimum order and lead time?

The minimum order and lead time depend on the garments and the fabric. Tell us how many people are on the team and when you need the uniforms, and Evidentelilás replies with feasibility and timing.

How does a uniform order start?

A uniform order starts with a message: the garments, the number of people and the date. The sample is approved first, then the factory produces the series, size by size.
